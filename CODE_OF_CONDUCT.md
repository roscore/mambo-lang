# Code of Conduct / 행동 강령

We want a welcoming, respectful project for contributors of every background and experience level. Be kind, assume good intent, give constructive technical feedback, and respect personal boundaries. Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or restrict participation when needed to keep the community safe. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainer contact or, if unavailable, open an issue asking for a private reporting channel without disclosing sensitive details. Maintainers will handle reports with care and share details only as needed to resolve them.
